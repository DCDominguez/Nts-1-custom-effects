## 2. Why

DC corrected earlier feedback: both note and delay were audible, with delay faint. After the suggested higher-MIX check, the delay was still below the desired level. DC approved the proposal to boost four main echoes about +6 dB before protection, retaining ghosts, bloom sends and phrase behavior. The proposed admission redesign remains on hold.

## 3. Changes

The only DSP change is a `1.995262315` multiplier on the left/right clear foreground sums, after foreground normalization and after bloom-send calculation, before wet summing and the existing wet guard. Voice event levels are unchanged, as are pitched ghost levels, bloom injection, capture/admission, scheduling, controls, TTL, memory allocations and ceilings. Manifest is 0.1-1. QA/package labels, current spec/system/history and CI's comparison check are updated.

## 4. Sound-quality intent

First and second answers should be substantially clearer; all four main echoes are louder. Avoid unnecessarily raising the atmospheric background. Preserve the existing phrase shape, stereo, finite decay and clean source. No change to limiter behavior was approved or made. The shared wet guard may attenuate ghosts/bloom along with foreground on hot material; pre-guard ghost levels and sends remain unchanged.

## 5. Runtime / headroom

No new buffers or voices. Two constant foreground multiplications per stereo sample are the intended runtime difference; actual instruction/code sizes are reported by the ARM build. No hardware CPU margin is claimed.

The existing wet guard remains .90 FS, followed by .8*MIX and the emergency output clamp. +6 dB is a pre-guard target, not a guaranteed final gain for every source. Ordinary input should avoid protection; hot-source attenuation is expected under the approved bounded change.
