# LATTICE ECHO

LATTICE ECHO is the `delfx` companion to LATTICE CORE.

Instead of echoing the complete stream continuously, it listens for event-like attacks in the incoming LATTICE texture and only promotes selected fragments into a longer feedback delay. The goal is to let one or two generated microloops become a secondary melodic trail behind the phrase.

## Controls

| NTS-1 | Role |
|---|---|
| TIME | **DISTANCE** — about 140 ms to 1.05 s echo spacing |
| DEPTH | **PICK** — 8 stepped selection-density rules, from rare events to every detected event |
| SHIFT+DEPTH | **MIX** — dry/wet balance |

Selected fragments are captured for roughly 40–95 ms. The feedback path is mildly darkened and cross-coupled so promoted fragments recede and converse across stereo rather than simply stacking louder repeats.
